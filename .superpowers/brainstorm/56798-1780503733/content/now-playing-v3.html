Play / pause artwork animation

The dashed box is the same fixed space in both states. When you pause, the cover art shrinks inside that space (and its shadow softens); on play it springs back to fill it. This is the Apple Music behavior — here exaggerated so the difference is visible.

— full space —
▶ Playing
art fills the frame · 100% · full shadow
⇄
— same space —
⏸ Paused
art shrinks, centered · softer shadow

(In the real app the shrink is subtler than this — closer to ~85% — but it animates, so it reads clearly in motion. I exaggerated it here for the still image.)
