## Sign-in required: yes

- **User name:** `app-review@deeppeace.space`
- **Password:** kept in the macOS Keychain (service `deep-appstore-reviewer`), never in this public repository. Read it with
  `security find-generic-password -s deep-appstore-reviewer -a app-review@deeppeace.space -w`
  and paste it into the field.

The account is a normal production member with onboarding finished.

Thank you for reviewing DEEP.

SIGN IN
Use the demo account above: on the welcome screen tap "Already have an account? Log in". It is a regular member account that has finished onboarding.

DEEP PREMIUM (auto-renewable subscription)
• Open the You tab → Settings (gear) → "Upgrade to DEEP Premium", or tap any sound marked with a lock in the Sounds tab.
• Monthly and yearly plans; the yearly plan includes a 7-day free trial for new members.
• A sandbox purchase unlocks the locked sounds immediately.
• Restore purchases and Manage subscription are in Settings.
• Terms of Service and Privacy Policy are linked on the paywall, in Settings, and on the sign-up screen.

GLOBAL PAUSE
A live group meditation that happens every day at 8:40 PM Bangkok time (13:40 UTC); its lobby opens at 8:30 PM. Outside that time, Home → Fuku's Lounge shows the next session and the peace messages members have left.

USER-GENERATED CONTENT (peace messages)
• Posting runs through a word filter; refused text is not published.
• Report: tap "…" on any message → Report → choose a reason. The message disappears for the reporter at once; three reports hide it for everyone until our team reviews it.
• Block: tap "…" → Block <name>. All of that member's messages disappear for you. You → Settings → Blocked members lists them, with Unblock.
• Our team reviews reported messages in an admin queue and can hide messages and suspend accounts.
• Contact: You → Settings → Contact us (support@deeppeace.space).

ACCOUNT DELETION
You → Settings → Delete account. It permanently deletes the account and its data, and reminds members that an App Store subscription must be cancelled separately.

OTHER
• Background audio is used only to keep sounds and sessions playing when the screen locks.
• No ads and no tracking.
• DEEP offers meditation and breathing for everyday calm; it is not medical care.
