Peace messages (user-generated content): members can leave a short message that others read in the Lounge. Messages pass an English and Thai word filter, and refused text is never published. Members accept the Terms of Service, which forbid objectionable content, when they create an account. Contact: Settings > Contact us (support@deeppeace.space).

Compassion: members earn hearts by practicing, up to a daily limit, and send them to causes to show which ones they would like DEEP to support. Hearts cannot be bought, sold or exchanged for money, and members never pay or donate anything in the app.

Account deletion: You > gear > Delete account. It permanently deletes the account and its data, and reminds members that an App Store subscription must be cancelled separately.

Other: background audio keeps sounds and sessions playing when the screen locks. Notifications are sent only if the member turns on Settings > Daily reminder. No ads, no tracking, no third-party sign-in. DEEP is for everyday calm; it is not medical care.
